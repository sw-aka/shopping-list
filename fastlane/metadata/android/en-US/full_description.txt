A small shopping list that works fully offline. It asks for no Android permissions and has no accounts, ads or tracking. Your list never leaves the device.

<b>The list</b>
<ul>
<li>Add, tick, reorder and remove items.</li>
<li>Restore a removed item with undo.</li>
<li>Share the list as text, with ticked and unticked boxes.</li>
</ul>

<b>Home-screen widget</b>
<ul>
<li>Resizable widget that shows the list on the home screen.</li>
<li>Tick an item to hide it. The header can undo up to three recent ticks.</li>
<li>The plus button opens a small add field over the home screen, without opening the app.</li>
</ul>

<b>Appearance</b>
<ul>
<li>Six colour palettes.</li>
<li>Light, dark or system mode.</li>
</ul>
